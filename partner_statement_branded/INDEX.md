## ✨ Key Features at a Glance

✅ **Professional Branding**
- Company logo in header
- Company footer with contact info
- Custom footer text per partner
- Print-optimized design

✅ **Smart Column Filtering**
- Hides: Journal, Account #, Due Date, Matching, Currency
- Shows: Date, Description, Reference, Amount, Balance

✅ **Easy Download**
- One-click from partner contact
- Professional PDF output
- Auto-attachment to record

✅ **Flexible Dating**
- Any date range support
- Defaults to current month
- Running balance calculation

✅ **Production Ready**
- Extensive documentation
- Full error handling
- Security-conscious
- Performance optimized
